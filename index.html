ICARUS Profile Editor

Client-Side Profile Modifier

Load Profile Save

Drop your Profile.json here, or

Can't find it? On Windows your save lives at:
%LocalAppData%\\Icarus\\Saved\\PlayerData\\<YourSteamID>\\Profile.json.

Press Win + R, paste %LocalAppData%\\Icarus\\Saved\\PlayerData, then open the folder named with your Steam ID 64.

Edit Profile

Meta-Resources

Set in-game currency and resource counts.

General Account Unlocks

These are account-wide unlock flags you can modify. They are not tied to specific missions.

Completed Missions

Toggle missions as completed or not. Missions that also grant unlock rewards show them under the name, and marking them completed grants access to those rewards; most listed rewards are skill or workshop blueprints access. Maps are organised into sub-tabs below.

Workshop Unlocks

Unlock workshop blueprints by category. Toggling one on adds its talent to your profile's Talents list (rank 1), the same way mission talents are recorded. Categories are organised into sub-tabs below.

Changes stay on this machine. Only the values you edit — MetaResources counts, UnlockedFlags entries, and Talents (via Completed Missions and Workshop Unlocks) — are modified; stats, slots, and everything else are preserved exactly as loaded.

To apply it, overwrite your original Profile.json with the downloaded file while ICARUS is running and on the title screen. The game doesn't lock the file, so you don't need to close or restart it.

A quick word of caution: to preserve the intended experience, consider saving this tool for bug recovery or post-game convenience. If you've already conquered the content and just want to bypass tedious grinding, it's a great time-saver — otherwise, it can easily take the fun out of early progression.
